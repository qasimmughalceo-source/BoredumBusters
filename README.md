# Boredum Busters

**Independent game studio · Smash boredum. Play more.**

Official website and brand assets for Boredum Busters. The name is always spelled **BOREDUM**, never "Boredom".

## The website

A single-page, interactive studio site with no build step and no frameworks. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, cPanel).

What's on the page:

- **Intro:** the orange fill loader counts to 100%, then the logo flies into the hero with a shockwave.
- **Hero:** the Burst mascot follows the cursor with its eyes, blinks, and talks in speech bubbles when clicked. Clicking it 10 times fast makes it burst into a baby that grows back.
- **Gaming interface:** a crosshair cursor with hit-markers, slash trails when you drag, an XP bar that tracks scroll, floating 3D-tilt cards, and a light/dark switch.
- **Bonus round with four mini-games:** Quack attack (duck shoot), Burst run (endless runner), Tank bust (tank defence with an upgrade shop) and Brick bust (breakout). A Random game button switches between them.
- **App Store and Google Play section.**
- **Pitch us a game:** a custom game-suggestion form.
- **Cheat code:** ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A on a keyboard, or hold Burst for one second on a phone to open a secret gamepad. Each activation fires one of three random cheats: 8-bit mode, Burst army or gravity glitch.

## Before going live

Search `index.html` for these placeholders and replace them:

| Placeholder | Replace with |
|---|---|
| `Boredumbusters@gmail.com` | Studio email address (appears in the hero and footer) |
| `+923224165064` | Phone or WhatsApp number (hero and footer) |
| `@Boredumbusters` | Social media handle |
| `Monopoly`, `The Summit`, `Comming soon` | Real game names in the "Games we're building" cards |
| `[50]%` | Build progress for each game card |
| `href="#" data-store="App Store"` | Your App Store link, replacing the `#` |
| `href="#" data-store="Google Play"` | Your Google Play link, replacing the `#` |

The two store buttons are styled in the brand look, not the official badges. Once the apps are live, download the official badges from Apple's and Google's marketing pages and swap them in.

### The pitch form

On GitHub Pages and other plain hosts the form validates and shows the visitor a summary, but **it does not save or send anything**. Saving only worked on the Claude-published version. To receive pitches, connect the form to a form service (for example Formspree, Basin or Google Forms) in the `PITCH FORM` section of the script in `index.html`.

## Brand kit

Everything is in `brand-kit/`. See `STRUCTURE.md` for the full file list.

- **Logo:** the Burst mark in three one-colour versions: white for dark backgrounds, black for light, orange as an accent.
- **Colours:** Ink Black `#0B0B0C`, Pure White `#FFFFFF`, Hot Orange `#FF6A00` (the only accent), Graphite `#1C1C1F`, Smoke `#8A8A93`.
- **Type:** Unbounded (headlines) and Manrope (body). Both are free Google Fonts.
- **Identity PDF:** a 10-page vector brand book that can be edited in Illustrator or Acrobat.
