Level 02 · Rules of play

How we make games

Every game we ship has to pass the same three checks before it leaves the studio.

A

Instant joy

If it takes more than three seconds to get, we cut it. You should be playing before the music loops.

B

Bold and loud

Big shapes, punchy color, sound you can feel. Nothing beige, nothing shy.

X

Made for everyone

Any age, any phone, any language. One hand on a bus or four players on a couch.
